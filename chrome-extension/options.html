ssf factories

A factory is the capability URL ssf-server logs, such as http://127.0.0.1:8787/<secret>/. Keep it private: it grants access to repository details and session summaries. The badge on github.com reads the factories saved here.

Saving asks Chrome for permission for that factory's address: that permission is what lets the extension read the factory and nothing else. Chrome's own prompt appears on the first save; a refused one leaves the factory saved and offers Allow on its row.
